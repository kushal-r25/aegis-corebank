## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the authority of a Tier-1 financial institution paired with the operational velocity of next-generation financial technology. Designed for critical financial operations, treasury management, compliance auditing, and multi-role customer portals, the aesthetic prioritizes unshakeable reliability, absolute legibility, and architectural rigor.

### Visual Aesthetic & Philosophy
- **Style Archetype:** Modern Corporate with Structured Precision. The interface relies on deliberate information density, razor-sharp 1px structural framing, and a restrained tonal palette that eliminates visual noise in high-stakes environments.
- **Audience & Context:** Corporate treasurers, retail banking customers, risk controllers, and compliance auditors managing high-volume capital flows and multi-currency ledgers.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires absolute fiscal security, decisive control, analytical clarity, and audited accountability.
- **Role Awareness:** System layouts accommodate distinct situational demands: dense analytical layouts for Auditors, swift transactional controls for Enterprise Admins, and streamlined, reassuring clarity for everyday Retail Customers.

## Colors

The color palette enforces institutional discipline. Color is never decorative; it is strictly semantic and functional, communicating state, lineage, ledger direction, and operational risk.

### Core Palette Application
- **Primary (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Deep Navy/Slate acts as the foundational structural tone, grounding headers, active navigation rails, key actionable labels, and primary button states.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#1E40AF` / `#2563EB`):** High-integrity Cobalt Blue used exclusively for system actions, focus rings, active interactive tabs, and hyperlinked navigation pathways.
- **Tertiary / Positive (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Precision Emerald designating credited transactions, settled assets, active accounts, and validated compliance passes.
- **Warning / Review (`#D97706` / `#F59E0B`):** Warm Amber designating pending settlements, escrow holds, manual review requests, and warning thresholds.
- **Destructive / Alert (`#DC2626` / `#EF4444`):** Disciplined Crimson designating debited balances, frozen accounts, compliance breaches, failed webhooks, and critical risk flags.
- **Surfaces & Borders:** Neutral canvas (`#F8FAFC`), elevated surfaces (`#FFFFFF`), structural hairline boundaries (`#E2E8F0`), high-contrast primary typography (`#0F172A`), and secondary metadata typography (`#64748B`). Meets or exceeds WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast ratios across all transactional data pairs.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes rapid vertical scanning, optical clarity at high information densities, and infallible numerical comparison.

- **Primary Typeface (IBM Plex Sans):** Anchors all standard reading planes, table headers, forms, and operational headlines. Its rational, industrial heritage reinforces corporate credibility and structured discipline.
- **Monospaced Data Typeface (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for quantitative and programmatic data points including account numbers, SWIFT/BIC codes, correlation IDs, timestamps, balances, and transaction ledgers. Monospace geometry prevents optical misalignment during tabular audits.
- **Tabular Figures & Metrics:** Every numerical monetary sum must enforce tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so decimal points and currency denominations align flush along vertical table columns.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** Large display headings are reserved for portfolio rollups and net asset values. System messages, table data, and metadata strictly operate across `body-md`, `body-sm`, and `label-numeric-*`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an uninterrupted 4px/8px incremental grid system designed to support data-dense dashboards, financial reporting workspaces, and high-frequency audit workflows.

### Layout Model
- **Grid Structure:** 12-column adaptive fluid grid with a maximum content canvas of 1440px for customer views and full-bleed viewport responsiveness with fixed sidebars for enterprise audit consoles.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, 32px page margins. Persistent 240px operational navigation sidebar with collateral collapsing to an 80px micro-icon rail.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 24px page margins. Collapsible secondary detail drawers.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px page margins. Sticky bottom action strips and stacked transaction ledgers replacing multi-column ledger tables.

### Density Tiers
- **Comfortable (Consumer Retail Portal):** Vertical row heights of 56px, form field paddings of 12px vertical.
- **Compact (Treasury / Admin / Audit):** Row heights of 40px, form field paddings of 8px vertical, dense 12px tabular grouping to maximize concurrent on-screen record density.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system rejects deep blur radii and exaggerated drop shadows in favor of structural, low-contrast architectural depth. Physical planes are distinguished through tonal separation and 1px crisp borders.

### Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#F8FAFC`):** The foundational backdrop for all page layouts.
- **Layer 1 Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Default card bodies, data tables, and navigation rails. Framed with a uniform 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Layer 2 Floating Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Flyout menus, search typeaheads, and role switchers. Defined by a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and a restrained ambient drop: `0px 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 3 Modals & Overlays (`#FFFFFF`):** Critical confirmation dialogs and transaction signing sheets. Supported by a 40% opacity Navy backdrop veil (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.40)`) and an intentional elevation outline: `0px 12px 32px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0px 2px 4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Dividers & Hairlines:** 1px borders using `#E2E8F0` cleanly segment table rows, metadata panels, and ledger groups. Hover states on interactive cards apply a 1px border shift to `#94A3B8` rather than artificial shadow expansion.

## Shapes

The design system employs a disciplined, subtle curvature (`roundedness: 1`). Soft 4px radii preserve an institutional, architectural character without feeling cold or dated.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radii (2px):** Badges, inline status tags, correlation code snippets, and mini checkboxes.
- **Base Radii (4px):** Primary interactive controls including input fields, regular buttons, segmented control tabs, and dropdown selectors.
- **Structural Radii (8px / `rounded-lg`):** Data tables, container cards, balance summary tiles, and role switcher overlays.
- **Modal Radii (12px / `rounded-xl`):** Flyout drawers and modal transaction dialogues.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for live status chips (`Active`, `Frozen`, `Review`) to contrast them against sharp, angular data containers.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary:** Background `#0F172A`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius, font weight 600. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#2563EB`. Active state shifts to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary / Action:** Background `#1E40AF`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for direct transactional executions (e.g., "Authorize Transfer", "Approve Wire").
- **Subtle / Outline:** Transparent background, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, text `#1E293B`. Hover shifts background to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Destructive:** Background `#DC2626`, text `#FFFFFF`. Reserved for high-risk operations ("Freeze Account", "Reject Transaction").

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Number Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#CBD5E1`, height 40px (compact) or 48px (standard), text `#0F172A`. Monospace font for monetary and reference inputs. Focus displays 1px `#2563EB` border with a 3px `#DBEAFE` halo.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Strict 4px rounded-corner square for checkboxes; circular for radios. Inactive border 1.5px `#94A3B8`, active fill `#0F172A` with white checkmark.

### Chips & Semantic Status Badges
Status indicators must always pair an explicit icon or dot glyph with an uppercase semantic label (`label-meta`).
- **Settled / Active / Credit:** Background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`, dot `#059669`.
- **Pending / In Review:** Background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`, dot `#D97706`.
- **Debited / High-Risk / Frozen:** Background `#FEF2F2`, border `#FECACA`, text `#991B1B`, dot `#DC2626`.
- **Informational / Audited:** Background `#EFF6FF`, border `#BFDBFE`, text `#1E40AF`, dot `#2563EB`.

### Data Tables & Transaction Ledgers
- **Header:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase `label-meta` typography in `#64748B`, 1px solid bottom divider `#E2E8F0`.
- **Row Anatomy:** Alternating or clean hover highlight `#F8FAFC`. Height 44px (Compact) or 56px (Standard). Left column reserves room for transactional flow icons (arrow upward red for debit, arrow downward green for credit). All monetary values right-aligned using `label-numeric-md`.

### Cards & Metrics
- **Balance Card:** Clean `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px `#E2E8F0` border. Top row displays account title and masked account number (`**** 4892` in JetBrains Mono). Center displays primary balance in `display-lg` with tabular numbers. Footer displays daily liquidity and cleared funds status.

### Role Switcher Context Bar
- A persistent context anchor positioned top-right or within the navigation header. Features a badge denoting current scope (`Customer`, `Admin`, `Auditor`). Switching roles triggers a subtle, systemic theme accent shift (e.g., Auditor context applies a dedicated amber security watermark and denser audit-trail metadata fields across all screens).
